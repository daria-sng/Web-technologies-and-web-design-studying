They carefully planned their tricks down to the smallest detail and presented them as spectacular shows for the public. During these performances, the magicians exposed criminals and brought them to justice.
In the latest film, Daniel met three new young magicians, Charlie, Bosco and June, and saw potential in them. He suggested that they work together to organize the biggest magic show ever, centered around a diamond. While they were preparing the show, they discovered that the owner of a mining company had used the diamond to launder money. She had planned to sell it and keep the profits for herself.
So the magicians asked for help to stop her. Finally, the young magicians agreed to join the team and achieved their goal.
